About the Clinic

One address for every specialist your smile will ever need.

Aadya Multispeciality Dental Clinic and Implant Center was built around a simple idea: patients shouldn't be bounced between addresses for connected treatment. General dentistry, implantology, cosmetic work, root canals, orthodontics and paediatric care all sit under the same roof in Besa, with sterilisation-first protocols and digital diagnostics behind every chair.

  • Digital X-ray & OPG diagnostics
  • Autoclave-sterilised instruments
  • Specialist-led implant planning
  • Same clinic, every follow-up
